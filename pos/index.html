POS

The counter screen your cashier works from, and the whole shift: opening the drawer, taking orders, taking payment, and closing.

The POS opens at the counter when a cashier signs in. A manager can use it too.

Opening the shift

Spicy will not let anyone take an order until a shift is open. If you sign in and see the opening form instead of the menu, that is why.

Count the money in the drawer and enter it for each payment method you are using. That figure is the starting point for the day's cash reconciliation, so get it right.

Only one shift can be open at a time, and only one cash method can be declared on it. If you take both notes and transfer, declare Cash as your one cash method and add the transfer method alongside it.

One drawer per shift. If your staff handle cash, the shift belongs to whoever opened it. They, or a manager, close it. This is what makes the count at closing meaningful.

Your open orders

The POS home screen lists the orders currently open on this shift, under Active orders, with two filters: Draft for orders not yet sent, and Sent to kitchen for orders the kitchen has. Click one to pick it back up.

You see your own orders. If you cannot see a colleague's, that is intended — each cashier works their own.

Taking an order

Click New order. The menu appears with your items grouped by category, and a search box.

Adding items

  • Click an item to add it straight to the order.
  • Items with sizes show as one tile with a price range, like "₦1,200 – ₦3,500". Click it and pick the size.
  • Items with add-ons open a list of extras. Pick what applies and the extras join the order at their own prices.
  • Greyed-out items are drinks that are out of stock. Food is never greyed out — a dish stays available even if you are low on its ingredients, because the kitchen controls usage.

Adjust a quantity, change an item, or remove it from the order panel on the right. Click the comment field on a line for instructions like "no onion" or "table 4" — they print on the ticket.

Order type and guests

Set Dine-In or Takeaway at the top of the order.

Use the guests stepper when guests are ordering separately. Each guest gets a card, and the items you add go to whoever is currently selected. The kitchen ticket groups by guest, so a table ordering as three separate bills still gets three sections on one ticket. Receipts group the same way.

Sending to the kitchen

Click Send when the order is complete. Spicy prints a ticket for the kitchen for food and a ticket for the bar for drinks.

After sending, the order locks. If something is wrong, cancel it and start again — you cannot edit an order the kitchen already has.

Taking payment

Click Pay. The balance is already filled in.

  • Cash — enter what the customer gave. Spicy works out the change.
  • Card or transfer — enter the amount and the reference if your restaurant requires one. A reference can only be used once, so a double-entry is caught.
  • Split payment — enter part on one method and the rest on another.

Payment must cover the full total. Only cash can be overpaid, because only cash gives change.

On success, the receipt prints and the sale is recorded. If the printer fails you get a warning but the sale still goes through — take the receipt another way and tell your manager.

Order history

History lists today's paid orders. Search by customer name or invoice number, or filter by payment method and order type.

Click any order to see its detail, reprint the receipt, or reprint a kitchen ticket. Cashiers see their own paid sales; managers see everything including returns and cancellations.

Cash going out during the shift

If cash leaves the drawer during service — transport, buying ice, a small repair — record it as a cash-out on the POS. Choose the method, the amount, and why.

Record these as they happen rather than at the end. A cash-out reduces what the system expects in the drawer, so recording it late makes the closing count look wrong when it is not.

Cashiers can record cash-outs. Only a manager can cancel one, and only while the shift is still open.

Cancelling an order

There are three different actions and you cannot use them interchangeably.

SituationWhat to do
Not yet sent to the kitchenDelete it
Sent, but not paidCancel it. The kitchen gets a cancellation ticket.
Already paidNothing at the POS. A manager processes a return.

Pick the wrong one and Spicy will stop you and say which to use.

Only a manager can cancel or return an order. If a customer wants their money back, call a manager.

Closing the shift

Click Close shift. Spicy shows what it expects before you enter anything.

  • Opening float — what you declared this morning.
  • Expected — what should be in the drawer now, after sales, change given, refunds, and cash-outs.
  • Counted — you fill this in.
  • Difference — the gap, if any.

Count the drawer properly and enter the figure. If there is a shortfall above your set threshold, a manager has to approve the close and write down what happened.

Finish or delete any open orders first. Spicy will not close a shift with orders still sitting in Draft.

A cash surplus is fine; a bank surplus is not. Spicy will refuse a transfer or card figure higher than expected — you cannot deposit money into the bank that you never took. Cash can genuinely come out more than expected, so that is allowed.

Common problems

"Open a shift before taking orders"

Nobody has opened a shift, or the previous one was not closed. Open the shift with the current float.

A drink is greyed out

It is out of stock, or already reserved on someone else's open order. Check Inventory → Stock balance. Drinks sold on open orders are held, so two cashiers cannot sell the same last bottle.

"Configure a production unit before sending"

A manager needs to finish Setup — the kitchen or bar production unit is missing or has no accounts assigned.

"Payment mode has no GL mapping"

A manager needs to give that payment method a ledger account. See Setup.

"Payment mode was not declared when the shift opened"

You turned on a payment method after opening the shift. It works from the next shift. This is intentional — the opening declares what should be in the drawer.

"This order was sent to the kitchen or bar"

The order is already sent, so it cannot be edited. Cancel it and take a new one.

My colleague's order is not showing

Each cashier sees only their own orders. Ask a manager if you need to see or change someone else's.
